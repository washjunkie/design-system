Colour

Six families, twelve steps each, generated in OKLCH. Step numbers mean the same thing in every family and in both themes — which is what lets a component be re-tinted without anyone re-checking contrast.

Aa — 4.6:1 ✓

--wj-accent-solid · aqua-9, the interactive teal

At 73% lightness the logo teal carries white text at about 2:1. Shipping it as the primary button would put the most-pressed control in the product below AA. Step 9 sits at 54% lightness for exactly this reason, and build.mjs --check re-proves it on every build.

Space & shape

A 4px grid, and two radius scales the register switches between. Operator surfaces are crisper; consumer surfaces are softer.

Space

Radius (operator)

Concentric rounding: an inner radius equals the outer radius minus the padding between them. A 16px card with 12px padding takes an 8px inner radius, not another 16 — matched radii at different insets read as a mistake.

Liquid Glass

Four optical properties, and all four have to be present: the backdrop refracts (blur), it saturates, light catches the rim, and the surface casts a shadow. What is not on that list is transparency.

Tier 1 · Veil — blur 14

Inline chrome. Sticky table headers, toolbars, floating chips.

Tier 2 · Pane — blur 24

Persistent structure. Nav rail, app header, tab bar.

Tier 3 · Shell — blur 40

A layer that arrived. Modals, sheets, popovers, the palette.

Solid

Tables, forms, long-form text, anything a person reads for minutes rather than glances at.

A hundred rows of 13px text over a blurred backdrop is unreadable, and it is the screen operators live on all day. The tiering is not a stylistic preference — it is what makes glass safe to adopt in a data product.

Three ways it turns itself off

No backdrop-filter

Falls back to the opaque glass floor with a real border. Nothing moves.

prefers-reduced-transparency

Blur goes entirely. The specular rim stays — it is what still communicates layering.

forced-colors

Real borders replace the material, so layer boundaries survive when colour is stripped.

Motion

Glass moves like something with mass in a viscous medium: it leaves quickly, glides, and stops dead. The one exception is --wj-ease-fluid, a sampled spring that overshoots about 4% — used where the material settles into place.
