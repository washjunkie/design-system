# Usage, requests & assets

*For anyone who is not us and wants to use the name or the marks — a provider
putting a sticker on their door, a directory listing the platform, a journalist,
an agency — and for whoever inside answers those requests. Open it before you
send a logo file to anybody.*

The other documents in this guide are about how the brand is made and who may act
as it. This one is **who may use it, how they ask, and where the files are.** It
has to work for someone who has read none of the others, so it ends with a page
they can act on alone. The honest state: the asset library is two SVGs and some
derived PNGs.

Its neighbour is [09 · Brand representation](09-brand-representation.md): **this
document governs the marks, that one governs the people.** A request to put the
logo on a door is answered here; a person standing at that door claiming to be
us is answered there.

## The name in text

The wordmark is drawn lowercase — there is no capital `W` in the file
([Identity system](03-identity-system.md)). That is artwork, not a spelling.
**In prose the name is `Wash Junkie`: two words, both capitals, always.**

| Rule | ✓ | ✗ | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Two words, two capitals | Wash Junkie | WashJunkie · washjunkie · Wash junkie | `washjunkie` is the domain and a code namespace, not the name |
| Never a verb | order through Wash Junkie | just Wash Junkie it · wash-junkied | A mark used as a verb becomes a generic word, and a generic word is not defensible |
| Never plural | two Wash Junkie outlets | two Wash Junkies | Pluralising turns the mark into a common noun for a category |
| Never possessive **in third-party copy** | the Wash Junkie platform | Wash Junkie's platform | As an adjective in front of a real noun, the mark stays a mark |
| Never hyphenated or broken | Wash&nbsp;Junkie | Wash-Junkie · a line break between the words | A headline that wraps mid-name reads as two things |
| No short form | Wash Junkie · the platform · the console | WJ | `wj-` is a code namespace — `--wj-accent-solid`, `wj-mark.svg`, `WashJunkieLogo`. It has never been a brand short form and must not become one |

This guide writes "Wash Junkie's own services" because there the name denotes the
**company**, a proper noun taking normal English. Require the adjective form in
third-party copy; it removes the ambiguity without anyone having to reason.

**Full name on first mention** in any document, page or post; after that *the
platform*, *the console*, *the app*. Surface names — Service Provider Console,
Wash Credit, Wash House, lowercase for ordinary domain words — are settled in
[Verbal identity](02-verbal-identity.md#naming).

### The symbol, and the attribution line

[Identity system](03-identity-system.md#misuse) already says: **do not add a ™ or
® on your own initiative.** Registration status is a legal fact, not a flourish.

- **™** is broadly used for an unregistered mark and **®** only where the mark is
  actually registered — but which symbol is permitted, and where, is
  jurisdictional, and nothing here is counsel's advice. Confirm both with legal
  for the jurisdictions we actually trade in (open decision #1).
- A symbol goes on the **first or most prominent mention only**, once per
  document — never on every occurrence.
- **Never inside the artwork.** Everything here is specified by height, and a
  glyph added to the lock-up changes what "height" measures: the drawn artwork
  inside the box shrinks while the number on the spec stays the same, so every
  floor in [03 · minimum
  sizes](03-identity-system.md#minimum-sizes-for-physical-reproduction) is being
  applied to a box that is no longer the artwork. Those floors are derived from
  the lock-up's own [two feature
  ratios](03-identity-system.md#the-geometry-that-governs-reproduction), which a
  ™ does not share. **TO SPECIFY:** if a symbol ever has to sit inside artwork,
  measure its stroke and its gap by the same method and derive its own floor —
  do not assume the lock-up's. A smudge attached to a legal claim.

> Wash Junkie and the Wash Junkie splash mark are trademarks of
> **TO SPECIFY: registered entity name**. Used with permission.

**TO SPECIFY:** the entity, the registration status, the jurisdictions. Until
they are known, no symbol and no attribution line ships. Editorial use needs
neither one.

## Third-party usage

The platform is a marketplace, so this is normal traffic, not an edge case.

| Who | Without asking | Written permission | Never |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Active provider** (a business on the platform) | The **name in plain text** on their own artefacts — door, receipt, site footer, social bio, and their own driver's shirt if they choose ([09's decision](09-brand-representation.md#decided--option-a) governs kit *we* specify, supply or catalogue) | Any use of the **mark** on their artefacts; any lock-up at all; any co-branded layout | Their brand replaced by ours; the name in their business name, domain, handle or app name; presenting Wash Junkie as the launderer |
| **Former provider** | Nothing | Nothing | Continued use of name or mark after they leave the platform |
| **Directory / aggregator** | The **name in plain text** to list or compare the platform | The **mark** in a listing, unmodified, at or above its floors | Any layout implying a partnership, endorsement or commercial relationship that does not exist |
| **Press and editorial** | The **mark** unmodified, in an approved colour version, in coverage of the company | A version we do not ship — mono, reversed, CMYK, high-res raster. Ask and we send it | Altering, animating, recolouring, or placing it so it reads as endorsing a product in the piece |
| **API integrator / developer** | "Works with Wash Junkie" **in text** | The mark in a marketplace listing, store page or comparison chart | Any icon, app name or splash screen that could be mistaken for ours |
| **Customer** | The name in plain text — a review, a post, a complaint | The mark | — |
| **Recruiter / job board** | The name in plain text | The mark on a listing we did not post | — |
| **Anyone printing merchandise for sale** | Nothing | Everything | Selling anything carrying the mark without a written agreement |

Four rules apply to **everyone**, including us:

1. **Wash Junkie does not wash anything.** A provider writing "Wash Junkie
   cleaned your shirt" misassigns the work and the liability. The platform
   connects and coordinates; the provider washes.
2. **The mark is never re-coloured to match anyone's brand** — the accent is the
   tenant's, the mark is ours ([White-label](../13-white-label.md)). Enforced in
   code on screen, by review off it.
3. **The field rules bind third parties too.** *Ink on the bright teal, white on
   the deep teal, never white on the bright teal.*
   [Full table](03-identity-system.md#which-mark-on-which-field).
4. **A mention is not a licence.** Being written about, listed or integrated with
   confers no right to the artwork.

> **The provider row is provisional.** Whether the platform mark appears on a
> provider's artefacts **at all** is [strategy open
> decision #8](01-strategy.md#open-decisions). Until that lands, the approved
> form of provider endorsement is the **name set in type**, not the mark.

**"Powered by Wash Junkie"** is set in type, not artwork. It is an endorsement of
the provider by the platform, so it sits where an endorsement sits: smaller than,
and after, the provider's own name
([co-branding](03-identity-system.md#co-branding-and-partner-lock-ups)).
**TO SPECIFY:** the exact wording, and whether it is optional, encouraged or
required. Blocked on the same decision — [strategy open decision
#8](01-strategy.md#open-decisions), tracked as #5 below.

## Whose brand leads: one principle, two media

One principle governs both, and the two media apply it differently enough to be
worth setting side by side.

| | In the console — [White-label](../13-white-label.md) | On the provider's premises — [Physical applications](06-physical-applications.md) |
|---|---|---|
| **What is theirs** | The accent hue: buttons, links, selection, focus, charts | The shopfront, the fascia, the staff garment, the name over the door — and the **driver**, wholly, with no mark of ours on the person ([09](09-brand-representation.md#the-driver-question)) |
| **What stays ours** | The mark, the glass, the semantic colours, the neutrals | The mark, wherever it appears, in an approved colour version at an approved size |
| **Who leads** | Their colour leads the chrome | Their brand leads the artefact |
| **What we are** | Infrastructure they run their shop on | An endorsement, second and smaller — **except on a driver, where we are absent** |
| **Why it stops there** | The mark is the only thing on a screenshot that tells a support agent which product they are looking at | A customer on their doorstep is buying from *them*. We do not take over a shopfront |

**Same principle, two media: the tenant gets the surface, the platform keeps its
own identification.** The Customer App is the case that proves it — the accent
never applies there at all, and a provider's logo appears as **content in a
slot**: no divider, no size-matching, exactly as a photograph would.

## Asking for something

There is no brand council — this document and a very short list of people. The
failure mode that follows is a request sitting unanswered rather than one going
to committee, so make it easy to answer. **Send it to one address**, not to
whoever you last spoke to. **TO SPECIFY:** which. `washjunkie.com` is already
the transactional sending domain — it is what
`MAILER_FROM` is set to in `api/.env`, and the exact local part is an
environment setting rather than a brand fact — so the domain is in hand; what
provisioning a receiving `brand@` alias actually takes is a question for whoever
runs the mail, not an assumption to make here.

| Request | Send |
|---|---|
| **Artwork for print or fabrication** | The artefact, the finished **height in mm**, the field colour, the process (offset, screen, embroidery, vinyl, laser, thermal), the substrate, the quantity |
| **Artwork for screen** | Where it appears, the slot's pixel size, and whether the background is light, dark, photographic or unknown |
| **A co-branded layout** | A flat with both marks placed, and who the audience is — that decides who leads |
| **Press** | The publication, the piece, the deadline |
| **Anything with a legal shape** — an agreement, a domain, a licence, a merchandise deal | The request in plain words, and expect it routed to legal rather than answered here |

What comes back is a **file plus a one-line rule** — never a hex in an email
([04](04-colour-and-materials.md)). Four questions need no request, only the right
page: *how small can it go?*
([03](03-identity-system.md#minimum-sizes-for-physical-reproduction)) · *what
colour on what background?*
([03](03-identity-system.md#which-mark-on-which-field)) · *can I put it on a van,
bag or shirt?*
([06](06-physical-applications.md#before-any-physical-order-ships)) · *can I use
it in a post or an ad?* ([07](07-digital-and-social.md)).

> **PROPOSAL — needs sign-off.** An answer within **five working days**. Where it
> needs a physical proof, add that proof's lead time and say so on day one rather
> than at the end.

Not a proposal: **nobody signs off a physical piece on a screen** — a screen
cannot show ink on kraft, thread on polyester, or a teal fascia under a sodium
lamp ([06](06-physical-applications.md#before-any-physical-order-ships)).

Sign-off holders are **TO SPECIFY** ([06
#11](06-physical-applications.md#open-decisions-and-to-specify)) and today may be
one person. Write down who anyway: "someone approved it" is how an unsigned proof
becomes four thousand bags.

### File naming

> **PROPOSAL — needs sign-off.**
> `wj-<artwork>-<version>-<colourspace>[-<size>].<ext>` — e.g.
> `wj-lockup-ink-rgb.svg` · `wj-mark-white-cmyk.eps` ·
> `wj-mark-1bit-576dot.png` · `wj-mark-teal-rgb-32px.png`

| Rule | Because |
|---|---|
| **`lockup` or `mark` — never `logo`** | "Logo" is ambiguous between the two, and `public/logo.svg` is the lock-up, which is the confusion itself |
| **Colour version in the name** — `teal`, `ink`, `white`, `1bit` | A supplier handed three files must know which is which without opening them |
| **Colourspace where it matters** — `rgb`, `cmyk`, `spot` | An RGB file arriving at a press is a reprint, and the filename is the last place anyone can catch it before the plate is made |
| **Size only when the file is size-specific** | A vector has no size. `-32px` and `-576dot` mean the raster was tuned for that target and must not be scaled |
| **Lowercase, hyphens, ASCII only** | These files travel to suppliers on Windows, through email, and into plotter software that mangles spaces and diacritics |
| **No dates, no `final`, no `v2-new`** | Version by content, not history. Git holds history; a filename that records it lies within a week |
| **No job or client name** | The asset is not per-job, and a per-job copy is how a stale mark survives three years |

Renaming `public/logo.svg` touches consumers and caches, so it is an open
decision below rather than an instruction here.

### Where artwork comes from

**[`assets/`](../../assets) is the source.** Everything else — the copies in
`web/public/`, the rasters, the React paths — is a copy or a build output. When
the artwork changes it changes in `assets/`, and every copy is regenerated in the
same change. Nothing edits a copy.

**A logo taken from a website, a slide, an email, a press article, a screenshot
or an image search is never the source.** Two of the three ways that bites are
true of files in this project right now: `wordmark.png` has an **opaque white
plate** — zero transparent pixels, checked — so on a dark surface you get a white
box with a logo in it; and `icon-512.png` is a **white mark on brand teal**, the
2.26:1 case, sitting in `web/public/` where it is publicly fetchable by anyone
helping themselves. The third happens off-project: a traced raster gives **a
wobbling contour and hundreds of redundant nodes**, which a cutter, engraver or
embroidery head reproduces faithfully ([03](03-identity-system.md#misuse)). So
when someone needs artwork, **send them a file** — not a link to a page.

## If you only read one page

1. **The name is `Wash Junkie`** in text — two words, two capitals. Never
   `WashJunkie`, never `WJ`, never as a verb.
2. **Never white on the bright teal.** *Ink on the bright teal, white on the deep
   teal, never white on the bright teal.* White is 2.26:1 on it and disappears in
   sunlight.
3. **Size by height, never width** — the width follows from the artwork's ratio.
4. **Leave `0.45 × height` clear** on all four sides. Nothing enters that box —
   not a rule, not a fold, not your logo.
5. **The mark never changes colour to match yours,** and is never stretched,
   skewed, rotated, mirrored, outlined, shadowed or redrawn.
6. **On your own premises, your brand leads.** Ours endorses, second and smaller.
7. **Get the file from us** — not from the website, a deck, or a screenshot.
8. **Ask before you print.** Every size, colour and process has a floor, and a
   proof on the real material is what a reprint is judged against.
